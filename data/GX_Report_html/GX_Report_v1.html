Limitations: This test is designed to screen for fetal chromosome abnormalities in singleton pregnancies that are at least 10 weeks gestation, as estimated by last menstrual period. However, it is important to note that this test does not rule out the possibility of other chromosomal or subchromosomal abnormalities, birth defects, or other conditions. Additionally, this test is not intended to identify pregnancies at risk for open neural tube defects. A negative test result does not mean that there are no chromosomal abnormalities present. There is a small chance that the test results may not reflect the chromosomes of the fetus, but may instead reflect chromosomal changes of the placenta or of the mother.

Methods: UCL laboratory preformed low pass whole genome sequencing from extracted cfDNA. The sequencing data was validated inhouse at UCL laboratory.

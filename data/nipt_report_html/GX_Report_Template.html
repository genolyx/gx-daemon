Methods and Limitations

Limitations: This test is designed to screen for fetal chromosome abnormalities in singleton pregnancies that are at least 10 weeks gestation, as estimated by last menstrual period. Microdeletion sensitivity is dependent on size and fetal fraction; the minimum detectable size is 0.5 MB at 4% fetal fraction. This test does not rule out other chromosomal or subchromosomal abnormalities, birth defects, or open neural tube defects. A negative result does not exclude all chromosomal abnormalities. There is a small chance results may reflect placental or maternal chromosomal changes rather than the fetus.

Methods: Low-pass whole-genome sequencing from extracted cell-free DNA. Sequencing data validated in-house. PPV performance calculated from amniocentesis and QF-PCR results. Where amniocentesis samples were insufficient, sensitivity was estimated from PPV.
